## Tier 2: ADE20K linear segmentation (runs/t2_seg/t2_ade20k.csv)

Frozen backbone, linear head (BatchNorm when the bn column is true, then a 1 x 1 convolution) on the last-layer patch tokens; one recipe and seed for every variant (scripts/tier2_ade20k.py; crop, iterations, batch and optimiser settings are columns of the CSV and of t2_ade20k_meta_<model>.json). mIoU, aAcc (pixel accuracy) and mAcc (mean class accuracy) in % on ADE20K validation.
Change vs original: mIoU of the variant minus that of the original model (points; negative is worse). vs SVD and vs random: mIoU of a Mirror variant minus that of the SVD or random variant of the same family and k (empty where that baseline was not run). Params: whole-model parameters as recorded; kept: kept over dense matrix parameters of the converted blocks.
V-exact rows: the model is rebuilt at the segmentation crop; the error is the largest relative error inside the span measured by the script on training crops (fp32), with its bar in brackets. Per-class IoU and accuracy: T_tier2_seg_classes.csv.
