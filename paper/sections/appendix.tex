\section{Pre-registration}
\label{app:prereg}

The pre-registration followed an exploratory phase on DINOv2-B in which we found the twins and developed the method.
We then wrote down every prediction of this paper with a numeric bar and committed it to a version-controlled
repository, which we will release with the code. Every number in the paper comes from runs started after that
commit. Mirror Surgery reuses the per-layer operators and twin pairings fitted in the exploratory phase on the same
2{,}000 fit images, after a check that the new measurement code reproduces them exactly (MUST-03). The thresholds were
not changed after the commit. Table~\ref{tab:prereg} lists every prediction with the measured value and the verdict,
scored by a script that reads the result files; the statements are quoted as registered, with our internal names
(Mirror-exact is Mirror Surgery, \texttt{vexact\_sN} its healed seed $N$, \texttt{best\_svdllm\_s0} healed SVD-LLM,
\texttt{dinov2\_vitb14\_reg} DINOv2-B/14 with registers, and the tax the redundant share of Section~\ref{sec:structure}).

A prediction is PENDING when an input is missing. CORE-01 (the controlled study of the cause), CORE-02 and CORE-05
P5.2 depended on a grid of small ViTs trained from scratch, which we dropped, as pre-registered, after the pilot failed
its gate (gate C, MUST-12) at two image sizes; CORE-01 T2 and T4 could be scored on the pilot runs alone and failed.
MUST-11 P11.2 is pending because the margins needed for the pre-registered rounding rule were not stored for the 422
pixels that differ. The upside items UP-01 to UP-05 were optional and not run; for UP-05 (speed) the even/odd form
measures 0.72 of the original's throughput, below the 1.1 bar. Operational changes during the runs (splitting long
jobs over parallel processes, reading images from disk instead of host memory, rerunning one job after a bug fix) are
logged with the code and do not change any result.

\label{app:structure}

Table~\ref{tab:structure} gives the structure measurements of Section~\ref{sec:structure} for every model. In the four

(DINOv2-B) points of $k$-NN accuracy, against 0.37, 1.39, 1.84 and 4.01 for the same projection onto left-right
symmetry.

\paragraph{Components and ablations.} Before healing, converting only the MLPs of the first six blocks keeps 76.60
(87.0\% of the parameters), only their attention 77.42 (94.3\%), and both 77.34 (80.6\%); for the full model the three

healing on the full model (62.10), but the result is not exactly equivariant and needs a basis change at every block
(7.5\% more parameters). Stacking a low-rank factorisation inside the even and odd blocks never beat low rank alone at
any of four budget points on DINOv2-B/14 with registers, DINOv2-L/14 with registers or DeiT~III. In float32,
the healed $k$-NN accuracies are 78.35 for Mirror Surgery and 78.08 for SVD-LLM (means of three seeds).

\paragraph{Efficiency.} On one H200 (bfloat16, \texttt{torch.compile}, median of 100 runs, one measurement per model,
images per second at batch 64): original 8{,}137; flip averaging 4{,}220; healed Mirror Surgery stored densely 8{,}167,
and in even/odd form 5{,}897. Int8 weight-only quantisation lowers $k$-NN accuracy by 0.006 points for Mirror Surgery
and 0.004 for the original; token merging with $r = 8$ by 0.18 and 0.34.

\section{Implementation details}
\label{app:details}

\paragraph{Data.} Images are resized to 256 pixels and centre-cropped to 224. From the ImageNet training set we draw,
per class and disjointly, three fit sets of 2 images per class, the $k$-NN bank (100 per class), the healing set (100
per class), a healing validation set (5 per class) and the method-selection set (10 per class). Healed models and
structure measurements use the first fit set; error bars before healing use all three. The 50{,}000 validation images
are used only for reporting.

\paragraph{Baselines.} All baselines compress the same matrices as Mirror Surgery after the same LayerNorm folding,
with statistics from the fit images, and are matched to its parameter count from above. SVD-LLM is the whitening step
of \citet{wang2025svdllm} (truncation in the basis whitened by the Cholesky factor of the input second moment) with one
damping value. ASVD-style scaling uses the mean absolute input to the power $\alpha \in \{0.25, 0.5, 1\}$. SliceGPT-style
slicing uses a PCA basis of the residual stream, per block or shared, computed from the original model and not updated
after slicing. FLAP-style pruning removes neurons and zeroes heads by a fluctuation score. ZipIt-style merging greedily
merges correlated units within one model. Hyperparameters are chosen by $k$-NN accuracy on the method-selection set.
When healed, low-rank baselines keep their rank, SliceGPT its width and pruned models their width.

\paragraph{Downstream protocols.} Linear probes use the class token concatenated with the mean patch token after the
final normalisation (for Mirror Surgery, the even parts) and multinomial logistic regression with weight decay chosen
on a held-out tenth of the training split. ADE20K uses a frozen backbone at 448 pixels, a batch-normalised linear head
trained for 8{,}000 iterations, and sliding-window evaluation on 2{,}000 validation images. CUB probes use 5-fold
cross-validation on the training split; facing labels come from the horizontal order of the beak and tail part
annotations. Some CUB images also occur in the ImageNet training set; this affects all models alike.
