\section{Mirror Surgery}
\label{sec:method}

Mirror Surgery converts a pretrained ViT into a network that is exactly equivariant to left-right mirroring and
stores each twin pair once. It has four steps: fit one mirror operator for the whole residual stream, project every
weight onto the equivariant form, rewrite the network in even and odd coordinates, and heal.

\paragraph{One operator for the stream.}
The per-layer operators of Equation~\eqref{eq:operator} differ from layer to layer, but in a residual network an
exact end-to-end symmetry needs a single operator $A^\star$ for the stream, since every block writes into the same
space. We fit $A^\star$ with Equation~\eqref{eq:operator} applied to $\sum_l C_l / \operatorname{tr}(G_l)$, where
$G_l = \sum x_l^\top x_l$, so that every layer contributes with equal energy. For DINOv2-B with registers, $A^\star$
has $d_+ = 700$ even and $d_- = 68$ odd directions. Before projecting, we fold every LayerNorm into an RMS
normalisation on a mean-centred stream and absorb the affine parameters and LayerScale into the adjacent linear maps,
as in SliceGPT~\citep{ashkboos2024slicegpt}; this changes no output, and afterwards exactness needs only that $A^\star$
is orthogonal.

\paragraph{Projection.}
Each weight is replaced by its average over the mirror, which is the closest exactly equivariant weight in Frobenius
norm. For an MLP with twin permutation $P$,
\begin{equation}
  W_1 \leftarrow \tfrac12\left(W_1 + P W_1 A^\star\right), \qquad
  W_2 \leftarrow \tfrac12\left(W_2 + A^\star W_2 P\right), \qquad
  b_1 \leftarrow \tfrac12\left(b_1 + P b_1\right), \qquad
  b_2 \leftarrow \tfrac12\left(b_2 + A^\star b_2\right).
  \label{eq:projection}
\end{equation}
Twin rows become tied ($w_j = A^\star w_i$), and a neuron that is its own twin keeps only its even part, since GELU is
not an odd function. In attention, heads either map to themselves or swap in pairs. For a head $h$ with partner $g$ we
require $W_Q^{g} A^\star = B_h W_Q^{h}$ and $W_K^{g} A^\star = B_h W_K^{h}$ with the same orthogonal $B_h$, so attention
logits are unchanged, and analogous constraints with a second map $C_h$ for the values and the output projection; the
head pairing and $B_h, C_h$ are fitted from the same mirrored activations. The patch embedding, the position
embeddings of the $16\times16$ grid, the class token and the register tokens are projected in the same way. Finally,
an \emph{even head} $y \mapsto y\,\tfrac12(I + A^\star)$ after the last normalisation keeps only the even part of each
output token, which makes the class-token feature exactly invariant to mirroring.

\paragraph{Even and odd coordinates.}
In the eigenbasis of $A^\star$ a stream vector splits into $x = (x_+, x_-)$. A twin pair has weights $(a_+, a_-)$ and
$(a_+, -a_-)$, so it can be computed as $u = a_+ \cdot x_+$, $v = a_- \cdot x_-$ with pre-activations $u+v$ and
$u-v$: one even and one odd dot product instead of two full ones. A self-twin needs only $c_+ \cdot x_+$. Counting the
remaining entries gives the number of free parameters of an equivariant $h \times d$ map,
\begin{equation}
  \tfrac12 \left( h d + \operatorname{tr}(P)\operatorname{tr}(A^\star) \right) = h_+ d_+ + h_- d_- ,
  \label{eq:count}
\end{equation}
where $h_+$ and $h_-$ are the numbers of even and odd hidden units; this is the character formula of representation
theory for the group of order two. A twin head pair likewise costs one head. Applied to every MLP and attention matrix
of DINOv2-B with registers, Equation~\eqref{eq:count} leaves 66.2M of the model's 86.6M parameters (76.5\%). On 144
MLP blocks of seven models, the count of the even/odd implementation matches the formula exactly.

\paragraph{Exactness.}
For a mirrored input the converted network's tokens equal the original tokens multiplied by $A^\star$ at every layer,
and its class-token feature is unchanged. In float64 the largest relative deviation over all 198 components on 1{,}000
validation images is $5.8\times10^{-14}$. In float32 it is $2.5\times10^{-5}$, and the $k$-NN prediction for every one
of the 50{,}000 ImageNet validation images is the same as for its mirror image.

\paragraph{Healing.}
The projection removes the part of each weight that broke the symmetry, which costs accuracy, especially in late
blocks where few directions are odd. We recover it with a short distillation from the original model: 780 steps
(two epochs) on 100{,}000 ImageNet training images with random crops and flips, batch 256, AdamW, and a mean-squared
loss on the final class and patch tokens and on the output of every block, each normalised by the size of its target.
Because the converted model is invariant, its final targets are the teacher's outputs averaged over the image and its
mirror image. The weights are updated freely and projected again with Equation~\eqref{eq:projection} after every step,
so the model stays exactly equivariant throughout. The learning rate is chosen from
$\{10^{-5}, 5\cdot10^{-5}, 10^{-4}\}$ by the distillation loss on 5{,}000 held-out training images.
