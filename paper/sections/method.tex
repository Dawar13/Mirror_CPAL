\section{Mirror Surgery}
\label{sec:method}

Mirror Surgery converts a pretrained ViT with GELU MLPs and learned position embeddings into a network that is
exactly equivariant to left-right mirroring. The conversion needs no training: an eigendecomposition and a matching
per layer, fitted on the activations of 2{,}000 images. A short distillation then recovers accuracy.

\paragraph{One operator for the stream.}
The per-layer operators of Equation~\eqref{eq:operator} differ from layer to layer, but in a residual network an exact
end-to-end symmetry needs a single operator $A^\star$ for the stream, since every block writes into the same space.
We fit $A^\star$ with Equation~\eqref{eq:operator} applied to $\sum_l C_l / \operatorname{tr}(G_l)$, where
$G_l = \sum x_l^\top x_l$, so that every layer contributes with equal energy. For DINOv2-B/14 with registers, $A^\star$
has $d_+ = 700$ even and $d_- = 68$ odd directions, far fewer odd directions than the early layers have on their own.
Before projecting, we fold every LayerNorm into an RMS normalisation of a mean-centred stream and absorb the affine
parameters and LayerScale into the adjacent linear maps, as in SliceGPT~\citep{ashkboos2024slicegpt}. This changes no
output, and afterwards exactness only requires $A^\star$ to be orthogonal.

\paragraph{Projection.}
Each weight is replaced by its average over the mirror, which is the closest exactly equivariant weight in Frobenius
norm. For an MLP with twin permutation $P$,
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
  W_1 &\leftarrow \tfrac12\left(W_1 + P W_1 A^\star\right), &\qquad b_1 &\leftarrow \tfrac12\left(b_1 + P b_1\right),\\
  W_2 &\leftarrow \tfrac12\left(W_2 + A^\star W_2 P\right), & b_2 &\leftarrow \tfrac12\left(b_2 + A^\star b_2\right).
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:projection}
\end{equation}
Twin rows become tied ($w_j = A^\star w_i$), and a neuron that is its own twin keeps only its even part, since GELU is
not an odd function. In attention, heads either map to themselves or swap in pairs. For a head $h$ with partner $g$ we
require $W_Q^{g} A^\star = B_h W_Q^{h}$ and $W_K^{g} A^\star = B_h W_K^{h}$, where $B_h$ is an orthogonal map within
the head's query and key space; using the same $B_h$ for queries and keys leaves the attention logits unchanged. A
second map $C_h$ plays the same role for the values and the output projection. The head pairing and the maps $B_h$
and $C_h$ are fitted from the same mirrored activations. The patch embedding, the position embeddings of the
$16\times16$ grid, the class token and the register tokens are projected in the same way. Finally, an \emph{even
projection} $y \mapsto y\,\tfrac12(I + A^\star)$ after the last normalisation keeps only the even part of each output
token, which makes the class-token feature exactly invariant to mirroring.

\paragraph{Even and odd coordinates.}
In the eigenbasis of $A^\star$ a stream vector splits into $x = (x_+, x_-)$, as in the mirror-equivariant ViTs that
\citet{bokman2025flopping} train from scratch. A twin pair has weights $(a_+, a_-)$ and $(a_+, -a_-)$, so it can be
computed as $u = a_+ \cdot x_+$ and $v = a_- \cdot x_-$, with pre-activations $u+v$ and $u-v$: one even and one odd
dot product instead of two full ones. A self-twin needs only $c_+ \cdot x_+$. Counting the remaining entries gives the
number of free parameters of an equivariant $h \times d$ map,
\begin{equation}
  \tfrac12 \left( h d + \operatorname{tr}(P)\operatorname{tr}(A^\star) \right) = h_+ d_+ + h_- d_- ,
  \label{eq:count}
\end{equation}
where $h_+$ and $h_-$ are the numbers of even and odd hidden units. A twin head pair likewise costs one head. Applied
to every MLP and attention matrix of DINOv2-B/14 with registers, Equation~\eqref{eq:count} leaves 66.2M of the
model's 86.6M parameters (76.5\%), and our even/odd implementation has exactly this many entries.

\paragraph{Exactness.}
For a mirrored input, the converted network's tokens equal the original tokens multiplied by $A^\star$ at every layer,
and its class-token feature is unchanged. In float64 the largest relative deviation over all 198 internal tensors on
1{,}000 validation images is $5.8\times10^{-14}$. In float32 it is $2.5\times10^{-5}$, above the $10^{-5}$ we had
pre-registered, yet the $k$-NN prediction of every one of the 50{,}000 ImageNet validation images equals that of its
mirror image. In bfloat16, rounding breaks the symmetry for about 2\% of images before healing and 0.27\% after.

\paragraph{Healing.}
The projection removes the part of each weight that broke the symmetry, which costs accuracy. We recover it with a
short distillation from the original model: 780 steps (two epochs) on 100{,}000 ImageNet training images with random
crops and flips, batch 256, AdamW, and a mean-squared loss on the final class and patch tokens and on the output of
every block, each normalised by the size of its target. The final targets are the teacher's outputs averaged over the
image and its mirror image. The weights are updated freely and projected again with Equation~\eqref{eq:projection}
after every step, so the model stays exactly equivariant throughout. The learning rate is chosen from
$\{10^{-5}, 5\cdot10^{-5}, 10^{-4}\}$ by the distillation loss on 5{,}000 held-out training images. Healing took 27
minutes for one seed on an H200 shared with other jobs.
