\section{Mirror Surgery}
\label{sec:method}

Mirror Surgery converts a pretrained ViT with GELU MLPs and learned position embeddings into a network that is exactly
equivariant to left-right mirroring (Figure~\ref{fig:method}). The conversion needs no training, only an
eigendecomposition and a matching per layer fitted on the activations of 2{,}000 images; a short distillation then
recovers accuracy. Proofs of the statements below are in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}.

\paragraph{One operator for the stream.}
In a residual network every block writes into the same space, so an exact end-to-end symmetry needs a single operator
$A^\star$ for the whole stream, shared by the structured early blocks and the nearly invariant late ones. We fit it with Equation~\eqref{eq:operator} applied to $\sum_l C_l / \operatorname{tr}(G_l)$,
where $G_l = \sum x_l^\top x_l$, so that every layer contributes with equal energy; by Lemma~\ref{lem:fit} this is the
best symmetric orthogonal operator for the pooled statistics. For DINOv2-B/14 with registers, $A^\star$ has $d_+ = 700$
even and $d_- = 68$ odd directions. Before projecting, we fold every LayerNorm into an RMS normalisation of a
mean-centred stream and absorb the affine parameters and LayerScale into the adjacent linear maps, as in
SliceGPT~\citep{ashkboos2024slicegpt}. This changes no output, and $A^\star$ fixes the all-ones vector, so the
centring commutes with it.

\paragraph{Projection.}
For an MLP with twin permutation $P$, the map $T(W) = P W A^\star$ is an involution on weight matrices, and a weight is
equivariant exactly when $T(W) = W$. We replace each weight by its mirror average.

\begin{proposition}[Nearest equivariant weights]\label{prop:projection}
$T$ is orthogonal for the Frobenius inner product, so $W_{\mathrm{eq}} = \tfrac12\,(W + P W A^\star)$ is the orthogonal
projection of $W$ onto the equivariant matrices: it is the closest equivariant matrix to $W$, and the breaking share
$s = \lVert W - W_{\mathrm{eq}}\rVert_F^2 / \lVert W\rVert_F^2$ of Section~\ref{sec:structure} is the squared relative
distance to that set.
\end{proposition}

We apply it to the first MLP layer with $T(W_1) = P W_1 A^\star$, to the second with $T(W_2) = A^\star W_2 P$, and to
the biases in the same way. Twin rows become tied ($w_j = A^\star w_i$), and a neuron that is its own twin keeps only
its even part, since GELU is not an odd function. In
attention, heads either map to themselves or swap in pairs. For a head $h$ with partner $g$ we require
$W_Q^{g} A^\star = B_h W_Q^{h}$ and $W_K^{g} A^\star = B_h W_K^{h}$, where $B_h$ is an orthogonal map within the head's
query and key space; using the same $B_h$ for queries and keys leaves every attention logit unchanged. A second map
$C_h$ plays the same role for the values and the output projection, and the head pairing and both maps are fitted from
the same mirrored activations. The patch embedding, the position embeddings of the $16\times16$ grid, the class token
and the register tokens are projected in the same way. Finally, an \emph{even projection}
$y \mapsto y\,\tfrac12(I + A^\star)$ after the last normalisation keeps only the even part of each output token.

\paragraph{Even and odd coordinates.}
In the eigenbasis of $A^\star$ a stream vector splits into $x = (x_+, x_-)$, as in the mirror-equivariant ViTs that
\citet{bokman2025flopping} train from scratch. A twin pair has weights $(a_+, a_-)$ and $(a_+, -a_-)$, so it can be
computed as $u = a_+ \cdot x_+$ and $v = a_- \cdot x_-$, with pre-activations $u+v$ and $u-v$: one even and one odd dot
product instead of two full ones. A self-twin needs only $c_+ \cdot x_+$.

\begin{proposition}[Parameter count and tax]\label{prop:count}
An $h\times d$ matrix is equivariant if and only if, in the eigenbases of $P$ and $A^\star$, it is block-diagonal with an
even block of size $h_+ \times d_+$ and an odd block of size $h_- \times d_-$. Its number of free parameters is
\begin{equation}
  h_+ d_+ + h_- d_- \;=\; \tfrac12 \left( h d + \operatorname{tr}(P)\operatorname{tr}(A^\star) \right),
  \qquad\text{so the tax is}\qquad
  1 - \frac{h_+ d_+ + h_- d_-}{hd} \;=\; \frac12 - \frac{\operatorname{tr}(P)\operatorname{tr}(A^\star)}{2hd}.
  \label{eq:count}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

Here $\operatorname{tr}(P)$ is the number of self-twins and $\operatorname{tr}(A^\star) = d_+ - d_-$. The tax is exactly one
half when every neuron has a twin or when the stream has as many odd as even directions, and it vanishes only when every
neuron is its own twin and the stream is entirely even. A twin head pair likewise costs one head. Applied to every MLP and
attention matrix of DINOv2-B/14 with registers, Equation~\eqref{eq:count} leaves 66.2M of the model's 86.6M parameters
(76.5\%), and our even/odd implementation has exactly this many entries.

\paragraph{Exactness.}
Let $\tilde{x}_l$ denote the tokens of the mirrored image at layer $l$, with the patch grid flipped back.

\begin{proposition}[Exact equivariance]\label{prop:exact}
After the projection, $\tilde{x}_l = x_l A^\star$ at every layer $l$ of the converted network, for every input image.
Consequently the class-token feature after the even projection is the same for an image and its mirror image.
\end{proposition}

Numerically, in float64 the largest relative deviation over all 198 internal tensors on 1{,}000 validation images is
$5.8\times10^{-14}$. In float32 it is $2.5\times10^{-5}$, above the $10^{-5}$ we had pre-registered, yet the $k$-NN
prediction of every one of the 50{,}000 ImageNet validation images equals that of its mirror image. In bfloat16,
rounding breaks the symmetry for about 2\% of images before healing and 0.27\% after.

\paragraph{Healing.}
The projection removes the part of each weight that broke the symmetry, which costs accuracy. We recover it with a short
distillation from the original model: 780 steps (two epochs) on 100{,}000 ImageNet training images with random crops and
flips, batch 256, AdamW, and a mean-squared loss on the final class and patch tokens and on the output of every block,
each normalised by the size of its target. The final targets are the teacher's outputs averaged over the image and its
mirror image. After every optimiser step the weights are projected again with Proposition~\ref{prop:projection}, so the
model stays exactly equivariant throughout. The learning rate is chosen from $\{10^{-5}, 5\cdot10^{-5}, 10^{-4}\}$ by the
distillation loss on 5{,}000 held-out training images. Healing took 27 minutes for one seed on an H200 shared with other
jobs.
