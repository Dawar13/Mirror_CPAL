\section{Limitations and outlook}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What did not work.}
Several of our pre-registered predictions failed, and they bound what the paper can claim
(Appendix~\ref{app:prereg}). Before healing, Mirror Surgery is the weaker compressor: on DINOv2-B it loses to SVD-LLM
at every size we tried, and in a zero-shot comparison on eight models it never beats SVD-LLM
(Appendix~\ref{app:extra}). It collapses on
SigLIP~2, the model with the weakest pairing among those we converted. Stacking a low-rank factorisation inside the even and odd blocks did
not beat low rank alone at any budget point. A per-layer rule that we froze in advance, to predict from weight
statistics alone whether Mirror Surgery or SVD would hurt a block less, was right on 67\% of 48 held-out blocks against
a target of 75\%. And the cause we originally proposed, that flip augmentation creates the pairing, failed in small
ViTs trained from scratch on ImageNet-100: the model trained with flips and the model trained without them had
similar early pairing, which Section~\ref{sec:structure} now explains.

\paragraph{Scope.}
All healed results are for one model, DINOv2-B/14 with registers, at one size (76.5\% of the parameters). Our
structure measurements cover 14 models, but the healed comparison does not, and the speed-up from the smaller weight
matrices depends on the kernels used. The pairing measure needs a few thousand images and a matching step per layer.

\paragraph{Outlook.}
Two directions follow directly from Section~\ref{sec:structure}. Early blocks are nearly symmetric under up-down
flips and 180-degree rotations as well as left-right flips, so converting them to the four-element group of all three
symmetries could remove more parameters while keeping the model exactly left-right equivariant. And the difference between vision-only and image-text models in deep left-right pairing is a concrete,
testable question about what training objectives preserve.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
Pretrained vision transformers spend a large part of their early capacity on mirror twins. Mirror Surgery turns this
duplication into an exactly flip-equivariant model with 23.5\% fewer parameters which, after a short heal, matches the
strongest structured compressor we tested and splits its features into what is in the image and which way it faces.
