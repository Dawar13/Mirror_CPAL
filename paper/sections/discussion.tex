\section{Limitations and outlook}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What did not work.}
Several pre-registered predictions failed, and they bound what we can claim (Appendix~\ref{app:prereg}). Before
healing, Mirror Surgery is the weaker compressor: on DINOv2-B/14 with registers it loses to SVD-LLM at every size we
tried, and in a comparison on the first half of eight models it never beats SVD-LLM (Appendix~\ref{app:extra}); it
collapses on SigLIP~2, the model with the weakest structure among those we converted. Stacking a low-rank factorisation
inside the even and odd blocks did not beat low rank alone at any budget point. A rule fixed in advance to predict,
from weight statistics alone, whether Mirror Surgery or SVD would hurt a block less was right on 67\% of 48 held-out
blocks, short of its 75\% target. Our controlled test of the cause of the pairing could not be run: in small ViTs
trained from scratch on ImageNet-100 at two image sizes, with and without flips, neither model formed many strong
twins, so the study stopped at its pre-registered gate.

\paragraph{Scope.}
All healed results are for one model, DINOv2-B/14 with registers, at one size, and the three healing seeds share one
fitted structure. The smaller even/odd form is slower than the original in our implementation (0.72 of its throughput
at batch 64), so the pre-registered speed bar of 1.1 times the original is not met; the fast mirror-equivariant ViTs
of \citet{bokman2025flopping} suggest that better kernels could change this. The conversion requires GELU MLPs and
learned position embeddings, so four of our 14 models (DINOv2-g, EVA-02, CLIP and DINOv3) cannot be converted as is,
and the converted model is tied to the position grid it was converted at (higher resolutions need the grid
re-projected, as we did for ADE20K). Exact invariance holds in float32; bfloat16 rounding breaks it for 0.27\% of
images, and quantisation or token merging break it further.

\paragraph{Outlook.}
Early blocks are about as close to up-down symmetry as to left-right symmetry. Converting them for the group
generated by both flips, which contains the 180-degree rotation, could remove more parameters while keeping the model
exactly left-right equivariant, much as larger groups are used when training from scratch~\citep{nordstrom2025quick}.
A causal test of our explanation of early pairing, for instance training with 90-degree rotations and checking whether
90-degree pairing appears, is a natural next step.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
Pretrained vision transformers spend a large part of their early capacity on mirror twins. Mirror Surgery turns this
duplication into an exactly flip-equivariant backbone with a flip-invariant class token which, after a short
distillation, matches the strongest structured compressor we tested and separates what is in an image from which way
it faces.
